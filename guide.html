How to Use & Features

1. Instant High-Res Hover
Move your mouse over any Instagram photo or feed video to open the full-resolution floating preview.
2. Scroll-Wheel Zoom (50%–400%)
While a preview is open, scroll your mouse wheel up or down to zoom closer into details.
3. One-Key Media Download
While hovering over a photo or video, press D (or your custom hotkey) to save the original file instantly.
4. Trigger Keys & Themes
Require holding Shift, Ctrl, or Alt before zooming, and switch between 4 glassmorphism themes.
5. Quick Dismiss
Move your cursor off the post or press Esc anytime to close the preview window.
